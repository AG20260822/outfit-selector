Let's see whether a small local AI model can recognise clothing in your photos.

1. Choose a photo

On iPhone, “Choose a photo” should let you select an existing picture from your Photos library. “Take a photo” opens the camera.

What does this mean?
This model identifies clothing regions in an image. It does not yet understand your personal style, weather, formality, or whether you like an item.

Privacy

The photograph is processed in your browser. It is not uploaded to an AI API for inference.

The AI model itself is downloaded from Hugging Face the first time you use the test and then can be cached by your browser. The model performs inference on your device.
