ABOUT THIS TEST
Your photograph is processed inside this browser. It is not uploaded to an AI API. The AI model files themselves are downloaded from Hugging Face when needed.

This model is designed primarily for clothing segmentation. It can recognise clothing regions such as trousers, shirts, dresses and jackets, but it is not expected to understand every fashion detail or reliably identify a specific brand.
